Review queue

How a claim moves

  1. SubmitWizard builds an RFC 8785 envelope for any tile and hashes it in your browser.
  2. GatekeepFive gates check envelope, bounding, census/timing, calibration/sensitivity, recovery limits.
  3. InspectAuditors interrogate curves, four-date imagery and the map before deciding.
  4. AnchorWallet-signed Sepolia transactions make reviews and challenges permanent.

Honest boundary: the browser verifies envelopes, method disclosure and anchored evidence. Pixel-level dNBR for a new tile is computed by the open CLI (izmir_dnbr.py pattern) and its outputs are hashed into the package — the UI never invents raster results.

    Core integrity design: a claim cannot outrun its evidence

    1. Census, not cherry-picksPaginated STAC to exhaustion, hash-logged. Omitted clearer/later scenes are detectable — İzmir v1 hid +1,917.6 ha that way.
    2. Sensitivity, not single points735.4 ha hides inside threshold choice; −1,703.6 ha inside one forgotten offset. Curves are mandatory disclosure.
    3. Uniqueness, not double countingRetirement serial blocks are interval-checked across the package; overlaps flag before credits move.
    4. Corrections, not erasuresChallenge → NEEDS_EVIDENCE → supersession stays on Sepolia forever, POST_HOC timing included.
